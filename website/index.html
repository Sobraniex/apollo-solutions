How we work

We set up the tool.
You get back to work.

If the software bill is growing faster than the business, we already built a replacement. We install it with you. We do not take custom jobs.

01

The tool should serve the work

If a program costs more time and money than it saves, it is the wrong program. We replace those.

02

It runs on your computer

03

What we do

  • Replace software that costs more than it returns
  • Install it with you. First day. Then work.
  • Your computer. Your files. Your numbers.
  • Only problems we see repeating in the market

What we don’t

  • Custom apps “for our company”
  • A new monthly licence to stay in the tool
  • Your data sitting on someone else’s server
  • A long sales process to get started

Mission

FAQ

Short answers

Are you an agency?

No. We don’t take briefs. We don’t quote every call. We pick a problem, build the tool, give it to the people who need it.

What does it cost?

No monthly fee, for now. We install it with you. If that changes, we say so straight — not through a paywall.

Why free?

Because bits are cheap and the subscription is not. The market charges rent. We don’t want to.

Can I commission an app?

No. A brief is not a problem. A problem repeats in the market. If the tool already exists — yes.

What is Hermes Dental?

First product. Local. Click the card. Not a medical device.

Where are you?

Slovenia. Firms stuck on expensive software. Each tool has its own site. This is the firm.
