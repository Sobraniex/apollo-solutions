How do you scope a project?

For local AI, we start with the task, hardware, data boundaries, people responsible for review, and support needed. A proposal follows only when the scope and likely benefit are clear.

Does an agent on my computer keep everything local?

That depends on the model and connected services. A local app can still use a remote AI provider. Our approach is to make those choices explicit: what runs locally, what is shared, and who has access.

What stays in human hands?

The goals, permissions, and decisions that need judgement. We design around visible actions and review appropriate to their consequences. AI should make it easier to notice and decide, with a person able to check its work.

Do I need to learn another complicated tool?

The aim is fewer steps in the tools you already use, with everyday questions you can type in your own words. A useful setup should make access and permitted actions clear. Your team still checks decisions that need human judgement.

Who is Hermes Dental for?

Hermes is currently focused on independent, single-chair dental practices using macOS. We review the practice’s workflow and requirements before recommending a setup. Multi-chair practices, Windows environments, and workflows that depend on automatic government-portal submissions are outside the current product scope.
